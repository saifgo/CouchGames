CouchPad is in **closed testing** on Google Play. Testers get the app before it's public, and every
tester counts: Google only lets us publish once enough people have tested for 14 days.

You need a Google account, and an Android TV (or Google TV, or a phone or tablet) signed in to that
same account.

## 1. Send us your Google account

Write to [{{email}}](mailto:{{email}}?subject=CouchPad%20tester) with the Google account (Gmail
address) that is signed in to your TV's Play Store. We add it to the testers list and reply when
you're in, usually within a day.

Only accounts on the list can open the test, so use the one on your TV, not just any address.

## 2. Accept the test invitation

Open the [testing page]({{testing}}) while signed in to the same account and choose
**Become a tester**.

If the page says the app isn't available to you, check that we've confirmed you're on the list,
then wait a few minutes and reload. Check that you're signed in with the right account (the
picture at the top right).

## 3. Install CouchPad

Either:

- **On the TV:** open the Play Store and search for **CouchPad**. The listing may say
  “(Early access)” or “Beta”.
- **From a phone or computer:** open [CouchPad on Google Play]({{play}}), choose **Install** and pick
  your TV from the device list.

It can take a few hours for a new tester to see the app. If it isn't there yet, try again later.

## 4. Keep it for 14 days

Please keep CouchPad installed and stay in the test for at least **14 days**. Open it now and then:
put a game folder in, scan the QR code with your phone and play something.

Google counts testers who stay opted in, so leaving early means the clock starts again for us.

## Send feedback

Anything is useful: a game that won't start, a phone that won't connect, a screen that looks wrong.
Write to [{{email}}](mailto:{{email}}?subject=CouchPad%20tester%20feedback) with your TV model and
what happened, or leave private feedback on the Play Store listing.

## Leaving the test

Open the [testing page]({{testing}}) and choose **Leave the program**. You can keep the app; you'll
switch to the public version once it's out.
