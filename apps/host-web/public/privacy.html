Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Short version: CouchPad has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. We do not collect, sell or share your personal data. Everything the app stores stays on your TV or your phone.

This policy covers CouchPad for Android TV (package com.tunisolutions.couch), the controller web page that phones open after scanning the QR code, and the CouchPad web version. "CouchPad", "we" and "us" mean the developer of CouchPad, Tuni Solutions. Contact details are at the end.

1. Data we collect

None. The app does not send personal data, usage statistics, crash reports or device identifiers to us. We run no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

2. Data stored on your devices

To work, CouchPad keeps the following on your own devices. It never leaves them unless stated in section 3.

DataWhereWhy
List of game files found in your game folders (file names only)TVShow your library
Copies of games from your game folders, made when you play themTV (cache)Emulators need a local file. Older copies are removed automatically
Save states, in-game saves, recently playedTVContinue where you left off
Settings and CouchPad Pro purchase statusTVRemember your choices; Pro works offline
Crash logs (device model, Android version, error details)TVTell you which emulator crashed. Never uploaded
Latency history (only if you turn it on in Settings)TVShow connection quality. Contains no names or game titles

Player names typed on phones are sent to the TV over your local network so they can be shown in the lobby. They are kept in memory for the session only.

3. Connections to other services

Some features connect to services we do not run. They receive your IP address, like any website you visit.

  • Google Play handles the CouchPad Pro purchase and payment. We only receive whether the purchase is valid. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • libretro thumbnails (thumbnails.libretro.com): box art is loaded from this public server. The request contains the game's title.
  • Your CouchPad server (optional): if you enter the address of a CouchPad server, the app loads your games from it. That server is run by you or whoever you got the address from, not by us.

4. Phones and the local network

Phones join by opening a web page served by the TV itself on your Wi-Fi. Button presses travel directly between the phone and the TV (WebRTC, which is encrypted, or a local WebSocket). They do not pass through our servers. The page may use the phone's vibration and, only if you turn on tilt controls, its motion sensors. It does not use the camera, microphone, location or contacts.

5. Permissions on the TV

  • Internet / network state / Wi-Fi state: let phones connect and show the join address.
  • Game folders: on Android 11 and newer, CouchPad only reads the folders you pick with the system folder picker. You can remove a folder in Settings. On Android 8–10, CouchPad asks for the storage permission to find roms folders. Only game and BIOS files are read.
  • Vibrate: rumble on game controllers.
  • TV home screen (EPG data): add "Continue playing" entries to the Android TV home screen.

6. Children

CouchPad is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

Because everything is on your devices, you control it:

  • TV: Settings → Apps → CouchPad → Clear data, or uninstall the app.
  • Phone: clear site data for the controller page in your browser.

We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete. Purchase records are kept by Google Play.

8. Web version

The browser version of CouchPad runs on a CouchPad server. If something crashes, a short error report (error message, page path, browser type) is sent to that same server, not to us. Anonymous latency summaries are sent there only if you turn them on.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
